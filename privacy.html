Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Stardock Incorporated, doing business as Stardock Marine (“Stardock,” “we,” “us”), is a mobile boat repair, restoration and boat sales business based in New Smyrna Beach, Florida. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us through stardockmarine.com or one of our ads, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

What you send us. When you fill out a form on this site, fill out a lead form on one of our Google or Facebook ads, or call, text or email us, we receive what you choose to give us:

  • your name
  • your phone number, and your email address if you provide one
  • where the boat is (a city, marina, ramp or address)
  • details about your boat and engine, and the problem or work you describe
  • how you prefer to be contacted, and any photos you text or email us

What is collected automatically. Like most websites, this site uses Google Analytics, the Google Ads tag and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. These services use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you view, the type of device and browser you use, your approximate location, and whether you tapped a Call or Text button or sent a form. The site also remembers in your browser, for up to 30 days, how you found us (for example a Google search or a Facebook ad); that note is included with a request you send so we know which ads are working.

How we use it

  • to respond to your repair, restoration or boat-sale inquiry by phone, text or email
  • to give you an estimate, schedule the work and do the job
  • to keep records of the work we do for our customers
  • to understand how people find us and to measure and improve our website and advertising

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your phone number or email address for marketing unrelated to your inquiry.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers the forms on this site to our email inbox
  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), for website analytics and advertising measurement
  • our email and phone providers, which carry your messages to us

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and customer records for as long as we need them to respond to you, do the work and maintain our business records, and then delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out add-on.

Children

This site is meant for adults who own or are buying a boat. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact us about your information

To ask a question about this policy, or to see, correct or delete your information, call or text us at (386) 227-6883 or send a message through our contact page.
