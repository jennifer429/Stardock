Common calls we get

Boat problems we fix on site

Boat won't start or won't turn over

Usually fuel, spark, a dead battery or a bad connection. We troubleshoot Mercury and Yamaha outboards at the dock and fix the cause on the spot when we can, so you're not towing the boat to a shop.

Tilt and trim not working

Power trim and tilt repair: motors, relays, switches, hydraulic pumps and leaking rams. A stuck-down outboard is one of our most common mobile calls.

Bilge pump or float switch failed

Bilge pump and float switch replacement, plus the wiring that usually failed with them, so the boat stays dry on the lift or at the dock.

Fuel system problems: stalling, surging, bad gas

Ethanol-damaged lines, clogged filters, primer bulbs, fuel pumps, carburetor and injector service. We clean up the fuel system and get the outboard running clean again.

Jet ski or PWC repair

Personal watercraft service and repair at your house or dock: no-starts, fuel, batteries, cooling and seasonal service.

Hull dings, gelcoat chips and fiberglass cracks

Color-matched gelcoat repair and fiberglass work done where the boat sits. Text us a photo of the damage and we'll tell you what it takes.
